Certification Services

Certification readiness, from gap assessment to audit day

We prepare you for the external audit and stay with you through it. Timelines below are indicative and depend on scope, size and current maturity.

ISO/IEC 27001:2022

3–6 months

Information security management system (ISMS). The most widely requested security certification in enterprise procurement.

  • Scope definition and Statement of Applicability
  • Risk assessment and treatment plan
  • Annex A control implementation support
  • Internal audit and management review
  • Stage 1 and Stage 2 audit support

SOC 2 Type I & Type II

2–4 months + observation

Attestation against the AICPA Trust Services Criteria. Common requirement for SaaS and service providers selling to US customers.

  • Trust Services Criteria scoping
  • Control design and system description
  • Evidence collection process for the observation period
  • Readiness assessment before the CPA firm engages
  • Auditor liaison and exception handling

ISO/IEC 42001

3–6 months

AI management system (AIMS). For organisations that build, deploy or procure AI and need to show responsible governance.

  • AI system inventory and scope
  • AI risk and impact assessments
  • AI policy and lifecycle controls
  • Integration with an existing ISO 27001 ISMS
  • Internal audit and certification audit support

ISO/IEC 27701

2–4 months

Privacy information management system (PIMS). Demonstrates privacy controls for controllers and processors.

  • Personal data inventory and processing records
  • Mapping to DPDP Act 2023 and GDPR obligations
  • Data protection impact assessments
  • Controller and processor control implementation
  • Audit support alongside ISO 27001

PCI DSS v4.0.1

3–6 months

Payment card data security standard for merchants and service providers that store, process or transmit cardholder data.

  • Cardholder data environment scoping and segmentation
  • Gap assessment against all 12 requirements
  • Targeted risk analyses for customised controls
  • SAQ completion support
  • QSA assessment readiness

A note on independence

The Risk Labs does not issue certificates or attestation reports. ISO certificates are issued by accredited certification bodies; SOC 2 reports are issued by licensed CPA firms; PCI DSS Reports on Compliance are issued by QSAs.

Keeping preparation and audit separate is a requirement of accreditation. It is also what makes your certificate credible to customers and regulators.

Our Approach

Four phases, one accountable team

The same team that scopes the work stays through the audit. You receive a status report each week and a decision log for anything that affects scope or cost.

  1. 01

    Scope & Assess

    Agree the certification scope. Run a gap assessment against the standard. Produce a prioritised remediation plan with effort estimates.

  2. 02

    Remediate & Implement

    Write policies that reflect how you work. Implement controls with your teams. Set up the evidence your auditor will ask for.

  3. 03

    Verify & Rehearse

    Run an internal audit and management review. Close nonconformities. Rehearse auditor interviews with control owners.

  4. 04

    Certify & Sustain

    Support you through the external audit. Help you respond to findings. Maintain the programme for surveillance and recertification.

FAQ

Common questions

Do you issue the certificate?

No. We prepare you and support you through the audit. The certificate is issued by an accredited certification body that you appoint. We can help you shortlist and brief suitable bodies.

How long does ISO/IEC 27001 certification take?

Typically three to six months from kick-off to Stage 2 audit for a small to mid-sized organisation. The main variables are scope, the number of sites and systems, and how many controls already operate.

What is the difference between SOC 2 Type I and Type II?

Type I reports on whether controls are suitably designed at a point in time. Type II also tests whether they operated effectively over an observation period, usually three to twelve months. Most enterprise customers expect Type II.

Can we pursue more than one standard at the same time?

Yes. We build a single control set mapped to each standard, so shared controls are implemented and evidenced once. ISO 27001 with ISO 27701 or ISO 42001 is a common combination.

How much time will our team need to commit?

Plan for a named internal owner at roughly one to two days per week, plus short sessions with control owners in IT, HR, engineering and legal. We take on documentation and coordination.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. We are headquartered in India and deliver engagements remotely for clients globally, with on-site visits where the scope requires them.
